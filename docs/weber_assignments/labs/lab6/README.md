# Labs 6 and 7 - Waypoint Logger and Pure Pursuit

One repository for two labs. Lab 6 records the waypoint CSV; Lab 7 drives it. Pure Pursuit needs the logger and its output, which is why they share a repository rather than each having one.

## Instructions

- **Lab 6 Sim** - [LOGGER-SIM.md](LOGGER-SIM.md)
- **Lab 6 Car** - [LOGGER-CAR.md](LOGGER-CAR.md)
- **Lab 7 Sim** - [PURSUIT-SIM.md](PURSUIT-SIM.md)
- **Lab 7 Car** - [PURSUIT-CAR.md](PURSUIT-CAR.md)

Each part is graded on its own and submitted on its own Canvas assignment. Every part
wants two links in the Canvas text box: the URL of this repository, and the URL of an
unlisted YouTube video of your code running. A video that has to be downloaded to
watch is not graded.

Record what you were asked to show, and put the link in Canvas rather than only in
`SUBMISSION.md` - the link in Canvas is what makes it one click to open while your
work is being graded.
